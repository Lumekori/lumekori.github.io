About Lumekori

What is Lumekori?

Lumekori is an independent software studio focused on building applications, intelligent tools, and digital experiences that are useful, clear, and able to evolve over time.

What we do

We design and develop our own software products. Our work combines application development, automation, integrations with digital services, and new ways of interacting with technology.

Our first product: Nao

Nao is an intelligent assistant currently in alpha. It is being built to help with everyday tasks, information, automation, and digital services, with regional integrations that can vary by country.

Meet NaoProducts

How we build

Lumekori starts with real problems before adding complexity. We test, learn, and continuously improve each product while giving every product its own support and documentation.
