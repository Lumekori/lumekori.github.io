⚠ Before you apply

Nao is in a very early development stage. You may encounter bugs, inaccurate responses, interruptions, and unfinished features. Do not rely on the app for critical tasks or share sensitive information during testing. Lumekori prioritizes privacy and security and uses safeguards to protect data; however, no software can be guaranteed free of vulnerabilities.

Request early access

Complete the form to request access to Nao Pre-Alpha. We'll personally review each application.

Your data and privacy

Lumekori Studio collects the nickname, date of birth, and email you submit only to review eligibility, manage Nao invitations, and contact you about your application. Applications are stored using Google Firebase infrastructure in the United States. Applicants cannot see other applicants' data. You may request information or deletion of your application at hello@lumekori.dev. Information is kept only as long as needed to run the testing program and meet applicable obligations.
