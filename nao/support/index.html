Nao · Support

Help with Nao.

Quick answers for the alpha phase and a direct channel to the product team.

I cannot access or install Nao from Google Play

During alpha, availability may be limited by country, account, or test type. Make sure you are using the account authorized for the test.

What languages does Nao support?

The interface currently includes English, Spanish, and Chinese. Feature availability may vary by language and region.

Why are some service features only available in Peru?

Utility bill integrations and certain services are developed by region. Nao may be available in more countries even when a specific local integration is not yet available there.

I found a bug

Include the app version, device model, Android version, steps to reproduce the issue, and, if possible, a screenshot that does not contain sensitive information.
