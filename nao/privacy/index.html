Nao Privacy Policy.

Alpha version. Last updated: October 13, 2026. This document must remain aligned with the actual behavior of each app version.

Information Nao uses

Nao may process information that you enter or authorize while using a feature, only to the extent necessary to provide the requested experience. Some features may require device permissions or identifiers associated with a service; these are requested when applicable.

Third-party integrations

When Nao interacts with third-party services, websites, or platforms, those services may apply their own privacy policies and terms. Lumekori does not control third-party privacy practices.

Permissions

Device permissions should be used for specific features and can be managed from Android. Not granting a permission may limit the feature that depends on it without necessarily preventing use of the rest of Nao.

Alpha phase

Nao remains in development. Features and data flows may change as the product evolves; when a change requires an update to this policy, the version published on this site will be the reference.

Contact

For privacy questions related to Nao: nao@lumekori.dev.
