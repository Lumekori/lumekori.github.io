Nao · Policies

Usage Policies.

Basic rules designed to protect users, third parties, and the services Nao may interact with.

Allowed use

  • Use Nao for legitimate tasks and queries.
  • Test alpha features and report bugs in good faith.
  • Use only accounts, data, and services that you are authorized to access.

Not allowed

  • Attempt to access accounts, data, or systems without authorization.
  • Use Nao for fraud, abuse, spam, or illegal activities.
  • Use automation to bypass CAPTCHA, security controls, access limits, or restrictions imposed by third-party services.
  • Deliberately interfere with the availability or security of a service.

User intervention

When a platform requires confirmation, authentication, CAPTCHA, payment, or another human action, Nao may pause the flow and ask you to complete that step.

Changes

These policies may be updated as new capabilities and integrations are added.
